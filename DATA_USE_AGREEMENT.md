# CWC EHR Data Use Agreement

Version 2.1 · 11 November 2026

## 1. Dataset and parties

This Data Use Agreement (Agreement) governs access to the controlled-access CWC EHR dataset deposited in OMIX under accession OMIX013035 and BioProject PRJCA051263. The data provider is Shandong Cancer Hospital and Institute, Shandong First Medical University and Shandong Academy of Medical Sciences. The applicant and every approved member of the applicant’s research team are Data Users under this Agreement.

The dataset comprises the deposited `patient_info`, `visit_record`, `diagnosis`, `lab_test`, and `socioeconomic_index` files and their accompanying documentation. The files contain pseudonymized longitudinal health information and indirect identifiers. Names, national identification numbers, telephone numbers, full addresses, and the source-to-pseudonym linkage material are excluded from the release.

## 2. Access

Access is provided through the OMIX controlled-access process. The data providers will approve access for every user who registers with verifiable identity information, submits an OMIX access request and accepts this Agreement. The same requirements apply to all applicants, irrespective of research question, nationality, institutional affiliation or type, and commercial or non-commercial status. Submitter authorization confirms completion of these uniform requirements and enables download. The project description records the intended use and is not used to select among applicants. Each Data User must use the data for the declared project and comply with the privacy and security obligations below.

## 3. Permitted use

Data Users may analyze the dataset for lawful research and development, prepare aggregate findings, publish research outputs, and share software or statistical models that do not disclose participant-level data. Each publication or public output must cite the dataset accession and associated Data Descriptor.

## 4. Privacy and confidentiality

Data Users agree to:

1. use the dataset only for the declared project;
2. refrain from attempting to identify, contact, or locate any participant;
3. refrain from linking the dataset with other information for the purpose of identifying a participant;
4. protect patient-level data and derived row-level data as confidential information;
5. report results in aggregate form and review small cells or unusual combinations before release;
6. notify the data provider and OMIX promptly after any suspected loss, unauthorized access, or disclosure.

## 5. Security

Data Users must store the dataset on access-controlled systems, use encryption during transfer and storage, limit access to named team members who have accepted this Agreement, maintain current security protections, and comply with applicable institutional and legal requirements. Credentials and download links must remain personal to the approved Data User.

## 6. Redistribution and collaboration

Data Users may not redistribute patient-level data, upload the dataset to another repository or cloud service, or provide access to an unregistered person. Collaborators who require patient-level data must obtain access through OMIX and accept this Agreement. Aggregate results, non-identifying figures, analysis code, and trained models may be shared when they do not expose participant-level information.

## 7. Retention and deletion

Data Users must retain the dataset only for the approved access period. At the end of the project, termination of access, or written request following a material breach, Data Users must securely delete all patient-level copies and confirm deletion when requested. Archival copies of aggregate publications, code, and non-identifying results may be retained.

## 8. Data quality and responsibility

The dataset is provided for research use. The data provider supplies the accompanying data dictionary and quality-control documentation and does not warrant fitness for a particular analysis. Data Users are responsible for selecting appropriate methods, respecting the documented limitations, and interpreting the data accurately.

## 9. Breach and termination

A material breach of this Agreement may result in suspension or termination of access. The data provider and OMIX may take proportionate steps required by institutional policy and applicable law to contain a breach and protect participants.

## 10. Acceptance

By accepting controlled access through OMIX, the Data User confirms that they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Agreement.
