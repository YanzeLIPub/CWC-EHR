# Laboratory metadata and cohort rule documentation

`outputs/lab_variable_metadata.csv` contains one row for each of the 771 released laboratory fields. Exact source labels, including prefixes, are preserved. Released units are standardized dimensionless scores. Counts and quantiles come from `lab_variable_qc.csv`; no patient-level records occur in this file.

The `source_unit_category` column classifies the physical quantity only where the analyte label supports an interpretation. The basis column distinguishes this annotation from a verified original hospital unit. Original hospital units and intervals are absent from the extract. The new assessment records a supported reference-interval linear model for 27 well-observed fields and consistency for two sparse protein label variants. Other fields are documented as spacing-compatible, constant-only or undetermined, with the supporting counts. These categories describe evidence for a model rather than confirmation of the original implementation. Parameters and inverse scripts are confidential.

All 408,976,950 cells represent 530,450 laboratory rows multiplied by 771 laboratory columns, including 384,077,273 empty cells. The 24,899,677 nonempty cells are finite numbers. The source-to-release comparison includes empty cells and found no mismatches. These checks establish faithful copying on the standardized scale, not validation of every original clinical concentration.

`outputs/eligibility_rule_dictionary.csv` records the eight categories, ICD-10 use and Chinese synonyms confirmed by SCH through the authors on 11 November 2026. The Chinese pattern columns have been renamed to reflect that confirmation. The term patterns are a compact representation, not executable hospital SQL. The itemized ICD-10 list and complete query implementation are not retained in the extract. The four released diagnosis sources cannot reconstruct the upstream complication screen. Heart failure qualifies independently of cardiomyopathy; at least one of eight categories is required and other diseases are permitted.

The companion workbook presents the same laboratory metadata and eligibility dictionary with filters and separate sheets for numerical profiles and explanatory metadata. CSV files remain the machine-readable reference. To rebuild them after regenerating the laboratory profiles, run `python document_metadata.py` from the archive root.

The additional all-field scale assessment is in `outputs/lab_scale_diagnostics.csv` and `outputs/lab_scale_summary.json`. `PROVENANCE.md` and `outputs/source_evidence_inventory.csv` identify the original and downstream materials examined. These checks confirm the release operation for all retained variables without assigning unsupported original transformations.

The transformation assessment and relationship evidence are in `outputs/lab_transformation_assessment.csv`, `lab_field_relationship_evidence.csv`, `lab_relationship_validation.csv`, `lab_model_comparison_aggregate.csv` and `lab_transformation_summary.json`. Public files contain aggregate counts and model descriptions; detailed mappings remain in the confidential editor attachment. The 269 constant fields are kept unchanged in the release.
